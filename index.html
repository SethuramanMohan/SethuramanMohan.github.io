About Me

Bridging HR & Technology

Seasoned HCM specialist with strong expertise in Solution Architecture, AI-native SaaS development, and multi-country HRMS delivery, enabling enterprises to modernize and optimize their HR technology landscape.

I design scalable HR solutions, enhance products using low-code / no-code platforms, and lead automation initiatives through RPA and analytics, driving configurable and automation-first SaaS architectures. I remain deeply interested in the future of workplace technology and how HR ecosystems will transform as automation, AI, and space-age innovations continue to evolve. 🚀
